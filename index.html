Asset & Prop Policy

Client-supplied assets: Provide all project-specific 3D models, custom mesh packs, and unique decorative assets, including thematic props, pets, branded items, and specialized furniture, before production begins.

Build services cover structural architecture, level layout, terrain shaping, and lighting composition. Custom 3D prop modeling beyond standard architectural geometry must be supplied by your team or quoted separately.

Project policy: Final tier placement is determined by the developer after reviewing the layout, project scale, zone count, and required level of detail. Each unique layout is scoped and priced as a separate build.
